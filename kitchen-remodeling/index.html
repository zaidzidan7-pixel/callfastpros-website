A Typical Kitchen Remodel Process

Every project is different, but most kitchen renovations generally follow a similar sequence of work.

1

Cabinets

Existing cabinets are emptied, cleaned, and either refaced, repainted, or replaced depending on the project scope.

2

Walls & Ceiling

Wall and ceiling surfaces are typically prepped and painted, often alongside cabinet work.

3

Countertops & Sink

New countertops and sink or faucet installation usually follow once painting and cabinet work are complete.

4

Appliances & Flooring

Flooring and appliance placement are generally finalized after the countertop installation.

5

Finishing Touches

Details like hardware, under-cabinet lighting, and backsplash tile are added to complete the space.
